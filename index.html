01 / About

Research interests

I am interested in the foundations of quantum theory and entanglement, and in their applications to mathematical physics, quantum computing, and quantum algorithms.

My research journey began with a PhD under the supervision of Dr. Ion Nechita, where I worked on quantum measurement compatibility and Bell nonlocality. I then joined David Elkouss’s group at OIST to work on computational entanglement theory and its applications.

More broadly, I enjoy exploring problems in mathematical physics and quantum information that may lead to new applications and have a wider impact.
